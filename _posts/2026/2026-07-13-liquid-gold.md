You poured for him a bowl of liquid gold <br>
Rich with life, and stories untold <br>
It bubbled to the rim, a meniscus it formed <br>
Of course with growth, uncertainty adorned. <br>

The trickle was gentle, small enough to stop. <br>
Small enough to account for every drop. <br>
It rolled off of the table and you respond <br>
Your hands, cupped together, embracing bond. <br>

You look to him, surely for a cup. <br>
But inside his heart, panic has bubbled up <br>
He paces, back and forth, bounces his fear. <br>
You watch, patiently, as line of oil grows near.  <br>

The burden is now on you, to bear the spill. <br>
A small mountain emerges from a tiny hill. <br>
The burden is now on you, to manage the pain. <br>
That will be your loss, and his feign. <br>

But you feel a pressure, it spins in your grasp. <br>
And you realize it is no longer oil that you clasp. <br>
Force of water, oil, and care, formed a scar. <br>
But you have accidentally created a star. <br>

It shines brightly, glows with elegance. <br>
Only existing because you took a chance. <br>
It smiles warmly, and you both know. <br>
That to the floor it eventually must go. <br>

Strong enough to hold a system together,  <br>
Resilient to emotional and physical weather <br>
He keeps his distance, hands at his side,  <br>
You cradle the strength that he could not provide.  <br>

It is brave to give when they can't meet you there,  <br>
To hold the gap with unwavering care.  <br>
But beauty is found in the fault and the spill,  <br>
In the accidents born when the panic falls still.  <br>

The signal goes out, but it echos right through,  <br>
A quiet reflection returning to you.  <br>
You will carry the star, and embody its glow <br>
A sovereign joy that continues to grow. <br>
